## When a commercial license is required

You need a commercial license if you:

- Integrate QERRA into a **closed-source or proprietary product**
- Deploy QERRA as part of a **commercial SaaS platform** or paid service
- Use QERRA in a **robotics product sold to customers**
- Use QERRA in **enterprise or institutional systems** without releasing
  your modifications under AGPL-3.0
- Want to use QERRA without the network-use disclosure requirements of
  AGPL-3.0

Academic research, personal projects, non-commercial open-source work,
and educational use remain **free** under AGPL-3.0.
