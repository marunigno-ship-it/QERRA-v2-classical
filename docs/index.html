What it is

QERRA-v2 Classical is a fully explainable, classical ethical evaluation engine. It analyses complex human situations against the SEMEV-12 framework — 12 immutable, human-centred ethical vectors — and returns a structured, fully traceable score with clear reasoning.

No neural networks. No black boxes. Every result shows exactly which vectors activated, why they fired, and delivers a transparent decision. The engine is designed for high-stakes environments where explainability and auditability are essential: robotics, human-AI collaboration, and institutional decision support.
